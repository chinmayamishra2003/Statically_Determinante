A Determinate Study Plan

No guesswork, no endless scrolling. Follow a clear four-phase plan built around the ESE syllabus, with revision cycles and mock-test checkpoints.

Four-Phase Approach

Build the Base

Cover the full syllabus once. Focus on concepts, derivations and standard textbook problems.

Practice PYQs

Solve 10 years of previous-year questions, topic by topic, and log every mistake.

Speed & Accuracy

Timed sectional tests. Aim to cut solving time while keeping accuracy above 85%.

Revise & Peak

Formula sheets, error log, and full-length mocks under exam conditions.

Common Questions

How many hours should I study daily?
Consistency beats volume. Four focused hours with active recall is more effective than eight passive hours.

Should I finish the syllabus before starting PYQs?
No. Solve PYQs for each topic immediately after studying it — this locks in the question pattern.

How many revisions are realistic?
Plan for at least three: one full, one focused on weak areas, and one formula-sheet-only pass in the final month.

Are these notes enough on their own?
They are a revision aid. Pair them with at least one standard textbook per subject and the official syllabus.
